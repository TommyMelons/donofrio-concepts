# D'Onofrio Homepage Concepts

Open `index.html` to review all four directions in one selector. Each concept can also be opened full screen from the selector.

## Concept 1: Editorial / Studio

This direction puts the work in the same visual class as a strong architecture practice. Oversized type, a restrained cream and red palette, large project photography, and an asymmetric portfolio grid make D'Onofrio feel precise and established. It is best for owners, agencies, engineers, and project partners who judge capability through the quality of completed work.

## Concept 2: Heritage / Family Business

This direction makes the D'Onofrio name the center of the story. Warm paper tones, navy, brick red, serif headlines, family history, and client quotes create a personal but serious presentation. It is best for clients who value continuity, direct accountability, and a contractor whose leadership stays close to the field.

## Concept 3: Industrial / Bold

This direction leads with scale, equipment, coverage, and measurable proof. The dark field, safety yellow, condensed type, capability ledger, operating map, and project index feel direct and operational. It is best for procurement teams, public agencies, utilities, and partners who need to qualify capacity quickly.

## Concept 4: Modern Minimal / Trades Meets Tech

This direction presents D'Onofrio as a modern operator without losing its construction identity. A clean interface, a featured project image, structured service system, project filters, and a clear four-step process make the company easy to understand. It is best for a broad mix of public and private clients and provides the strongest base for a larger future site.

## Recommendation

Concept 4 is the recommended direction, pending client confirmation of credentials, project years, and testimonials.

- It balances family credibility, field capability, and modern project control without pushing any one idea too far.
- It gives project owners the fastest route to services, work, qualifications, and an estimate.
- Its modular system can grow into project case studies, equipment pages, safety content, careers, and procurement resources.

## Needed before the next pass

- Approved, high-resolution project photos with project names, locations, completion years, scopes, and photographer rights.
- Approved family, leadership, crew, yard, fleet, equipment, and active site photography.
- Permission to use client names, logos, and testimonials.
- Confirmed phone number, email address, license numbers, insurance language, bonding capacity, union affiliations, and certifications.
- Confirmed service areas, states, boroughs, waterways, and emergency response coverage.
- Verified company metrics, including fleet size, project value, safety record, and workforce count.
- Final service priorities and the type of estimate or qualification request the contact form should collect.

Project sections use names and photography published on D'Onofrio's current website. Remaining stock hero and crew photography is representative and should be replaced with approved D'Onofrio assets before launch.
